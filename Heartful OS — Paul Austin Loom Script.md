## 0:00 – 0:35 · Open (face, no screen yet)

> Hey Paul — thanks for asking to see this. I'm going to keep it to five minutes and actually walk you through the product rather than pitch you.
>
> You know this better than I do: the work has a shape. Intake, preparation, journey day, the check-in right after, then integration. It's an arc, not a series of appointments.
>
> **But almost every facilitator I know is running that arc out of Google Docs, a calendar, and a text thread. Everybody rebuilds the same system from scratch, and everybody rebuilds it badly.**
>
> So that's what this is. The arc, built into the software.

*Don't explain the modality to Paul. He teaches it. Name the shape and move.*

## 0:35 – 1:05 · The model
**Screen: client record, top of page — the phase pills**

> Here's the whole idea in one screen. Every client sits somewhere on this arc — Intake, Preparation, Journey Day, the 12-hour check-in, Integration 1, Integration 2.
>
> **These aren't labels. Each one is a real workspace with its own forms, its own prep, its own risk profile.** The software knows which phase this person is in, and everything else organizes around that.

*Trace the six pills slowly with your cursor as you name them. This is your thesis — don't rush it.*

**Screen: switch windows to the portal**

> Last thing, and it's the part people don't expect.
>
> Every practice tool serves the practitioner. **This one also serves the client.** Marcus logs in and sees the same arc I see — where he is, what's coming, what's his to do.
