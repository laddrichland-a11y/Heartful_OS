# Heartful OS — 3:00 Promo Video
**Capture guide + Canva assembly · practitioner audience · 16:9 · on-screen text, no live narration**

You are recording raw screen clips, then assembling them in Canva. Record every clip **twice as long as you need**. Trimming in Canva is free; re-recording is not.

---

# PART 1 — Before you record

## 1. Set up the app
1. Deploy current and open the **live site** (`heartful-os.netlify.app`), not `localhost:3000`. A `localhost` address bar reads as a prototype.
2. **Log in ahead of time.** Never record a login screen.
3. Pick one **hero client** and confirm they have: sessions across several phases, at least two AI summaries, a message thread with replies on both sides, a completed intake form, and a populated History tab. An empty History tab kills the strongest shot in the video.
4. Set the hero client's current phase to **Integration 1** so the progress bar reads as mid-journey, not just-started.
5. **Pre-generate one Prepare Me briefing** for the session you'll show. You'll record the button click, but you want the result to appear fast — see Clip 08.
6. Open a **second browser window** logged into the client portal as that same client. Switching windows beats logging out mid-shoot.

## 2. Set up the screen
1. Do Not Disturb on. Quit Slack, Mail, Messages.
2. Hide the bookmarks bar (`Cmd + Shift + B`).
3. Resize the browser window to a clean 16:9 shape — roughly 1600 × 900 or 1920 × 1080.
4. Browser zoom to **110%** (`Cmd + +` once). Text has to survive video compression.
5. Empty the desktop behind the window, or just don't include it in frame.

## 3. Set up the recorder (macOS)
1. `Cmd + Shift + 5` → **Options**:
   - Save to: a new folder called `Promo Raw`
   - Timer: **None**
   - Microphone: **Off** (no narration in this cut)
   - **Show Mouse Clicks in Recording: ON** ← this is what makes clicks legible without a voiceover
2. Choose **Record Selected Window** and click the browser window. This crops out the desktop, menu bar, and dock automatically.
3. Test-record 5 seconds and play it back before shooting the real thing.

## 4. How to move the mouse
This is the single biggest difference between amateur and professional screen footage:
- Move **slowly and in straight lines.** No hunting, no circling, no jitter.
- **Pause 1 full second** before every click and 2 seconds after the page settles. Those pauses are your edit points.
- Scroll with **small, even strokes.** A trackpad flick that snaps to the bottom is unusable.
- If you fumble, don't stop — pause 3 seconds and redo the move in the same take.

## 5. Two things not to do on camera
- **Do not click a session from the Dashboard.** That link currently lands on the client's Documents tab instead of the session. Navigate via Clients → hero client → Sessions instead.
- **Do not put the sidebar "AI Copilot" and the client-record "AI Copilot" tab in the same sequence.** They're different tools with the same name, and back-to-back it looks like a bug. Show only the client-record one.

### Clip 01 — `01-dashboard.mov`
**Start:** `/dashboard`, already loaded, mouse parked bottom-right.
**Do:** Hold still 2s. Move slowly to hover **Active Clients**, pause 2s. Move to **Upcoming Sessions**, pause 2s. Move to **Outstanding Forms**, pause 2s. Move to **Messages**, pause 2s. Park. Do not click anything.
**Why it's here:** the "this is your morning" shot.

### Clip 02 — `02-clients-list.mov`
**Start:** `/dashboard`.
**Do:** Click **Clients** in the sidebar. Let the list render, hold 3s. Move to your hero client's row, pause 1s, click. Hold 3s on the loaded record. Stop.

### Clip 03 — `03-client-header.mov`
**Start:** hero client record, scrolled to top.
**Do:** Hold 3s on the full header. Slowly trace across: name → status badge → payment summary → portal status → emergency contact. Pause 1s on each. Park. No clicks.
**Why it's here:** everything on one screen, no hunting.

### Clip 04 — `04-phase-pills.mov` ⭐ protected
**Start:** same view, header visible.
**Do:** Hold 2s on the **milestone progress bar**. Then move slowly left-to-right across the six phase pills — Intake → Preparation → Journey Day → 12hr Check-In → Integration 1 → Integration 2 — pausing 1.5s on each. Park on the highlighted current phase. Hold 3s. Stop.
**Why it's here:** this is the whole thesis in one visual. If a shot has to be perfect, it's this one. Record it three times.

### Clip 05 — `05-phase-workspace.mov`
**Start:** same view.
**Do:** Click the **Preparation** pill. Let the workspace load, hold 4s. Scroll down slowly one screen. Scroll back up. Stop.
**Why it's here:** proves each phase is a real workspace, not a label.

### Clip 06 — `06-action-card.mov`
**Start:** back on the client record (browser back), Action Card visible.
**Do:** Hold 3s on the tile row. Hover each tile 1.5s: Upcoming Sessions → Outstanding Forms → Open Tasks → Portal Assignments. Park. No clicks.

### Clip 07 — `07-documents-tab.mov`
**Start:** client record.
**Do:** Click the **Documents** tab. Hold 3s. Scroll slowly through the stage groupings — Intake, Preparation, Integration 1, Integration 2 — so the grouping headers read clearly. Scroll back to top. Stop.

### Clip 08 — `08-prepare-me.mov` ⭐ protected
**Start:** client record → **Sessions** tab.
**Do:** Click a session with real history behind it. On the session detail page, hold 2s, then click **Prepare Me**. Keep rolling through the entire generation — even if it takes 40 seconds. When the briefing appears, hold 3s, then scroll through it slowly. Stop.
**In Canva:** speed the generation portion up to 4× or 8× so the wait reads as a beat, not dead air. If it took longer than ~15s even sped up, cut it and use your pre-generated briefing instead — cut from the click straight to the finished briefing.
**Why it's here:** this is the payoff shot. The briefing has to be visibly specific to this client, not generic. If the text on screen could describe anyone, pick a different session.

### Clip 09 — `09-journey-summary.mov`
**Start:** client record → **Journey & AI** tab.
**Do:** Hold 3s. Scroll slowly through the **Living Journey Summary**. Stop.

### Clip 10 — `10-history-tab.mov` ⭐ protected
**Start:** client record → **History** tab.
**Do:** Hold 3s at the top. Then one **continuous, slow, even scroll** all the way down the audit trail. Do not stop mid-scroll. Reach the bottom, hold 2s. Stop.
**In Canva:** speed to 1.5–2× and let it run under the caption. A long unbroken scroll of real entries is the most persuasive shot in the video — it can't be faked and it says "this is a real clinical record."

### Clip 11 — `11-calendar.mov`
**Start:** sidebar → **Calendar**.
**Do:** Hold 4s on the month view. Click forward one month, hold 3s. Click back. Stop. B-roll — nice to have, first to cut if you're long.

### Clip 12 — `12-portal-home.mov`
**Switch to the portal window.**
**Start:** portal **Home**.
**Do:** Hold 3s on the journey progress bar. Move slowly down to Action Items, pause on each. Park. No clicks.
**Why it's here:** the reveal that the client sees the same arc.

### Clip 13 — `13-portal-checkin.mov` ⭐ protected
**Start:** portal → **Forms & Check-Ins**.
**Do:** Hold 3s on the list. Click the **12-hour check-in**. Let the form load, hold 3s. Scroll down through the questions slowly. Fill in one or two answers so it's visibly interactive. Stop before submitting.
**Why it's here:** the 12-hour check-in is the highest-clinical-value moment and the one most likely to be missed without a system. This is the clip that makes practitioners want it.

### Clip 14 — `14-portal-messages.mov`
**Start:** portal → **Messages**.
**Do:** Hold 3s on the thread. Scroll up through a few exchanges. Scroll back down. Type a short message into the box — don't send. Stop.

# PART 3 — Assembling in Canva

## Brand assets you already have
Pull these from the project folder before you start:
- `Heartful OS Logo.svg` and `Heartful OS Icon.svg`
- `Loom Backgrounds/Heartful-OS-Loom-BG-1920x1080-logo-left.png` — use as the title-card background so the cards match the app

**Colors** (set these as a Brand Kit in Canva so every text element is one click):

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Terracotta (accent, highlights) | `#b5663f` |
| Ink (body text) | `#2b2420` |
| Warm sand (card backgrounds) | `#f3e6d8` |
| Off-white (page background) | `#fbf5ef` |
| Muted (secondary text) | `#948572` |

## Steps
1. **Create design → Video → 1920 × 1080.**
2. **Uploads → upload all 14 clips.** They'll sort by filename, which is why they're numbered.
3. Build the timeline in the order below. Add each clip, then trim it to its target length by dragging the clip edges.
4. **Title cards:** for each one, add a new page, set the background to the Loom BG image, and add text. Keep every card to **one line, ≤ 8 words**, ink or terracotta, large.
5. **Captions over footage:** add a text box in the lower third. Warm-sand rounded rectangle behind it at ~90% opacity so it stays readable over the UI. One line each. Same position on every clip — do not let captions move around.
6. **Transitions:** apply **Dissolve, 0.3s** between every clip, everywhere, no exceptions. Mixing transition styles is the fastest way to look homemade.
7. **Speed adjustments:** Clip 08 → 4× on the generation portion. Clip 10 → 1.5–2×. Everything else 1×.
8. **Music:** Canva audio library → search *calm, ambient, cinematic*. Set volume to **15–20%**. Apply **fade out** over the last 3 seconds.
9. **Canva AI voice (optional):** if you decide to add one, keep the on-screen text as-is and let the voice read the same lines. Don't write separate voice copy — the mismatch is distracting.
10. **Export:** Share → Download → **MP4 Video, 1080p**.

# PART 4 — Check before you publish

- [ ] Play it once **muted** — does the story land on the text alone?
- [ ] Play it once on your **phone** — is every caption readable at that size?
- [ ] No real client names, emails, or phone numbers visible in any frame. Scrub frame by frame through Clips 01, 02, 03, 10, and 14.
- [ ] No `localhost`, no dev tools, no browser notifications, no other tabs' titles.
- [ ] Captions all sit in the same lower-third position.
- [ ] Music fades out; it doesn't just stop.
- [ ] Every transition is the same dissolve.
- [ ] Final file is 1080p MP4 and under 3:05.

**Record the whole set twice.** The second pass is always cleaner and takes half as long.
